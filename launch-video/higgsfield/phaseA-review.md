## H1 — obsidian plate

| | H1-A ✅ | H1-B |
|---|---|---|
| Content | Smoked glass edge at ~63% x, a single thin **horizontal** slit of light across the glass toward the right edge | Same glass edge, soft **vertical** light band in the right third, more floor texture |
| Negative space | Left ~60% pure black — copy area clean | Same |
| Fit | The single line of light is the film's idea made visual: 「一行」. Strong graphic line, reads as Swiss rather than atmospheric | Moodier but generic; the bright band competes with the copy for attention |
| Motion potential | Slit can drift / breathe along the glass — exactly V1's prompt | Band shimmer only |
| Risks | Slit hits the right frame edge at high brightness — keep the AE 103% push centred so it doesn't clip harder | Floor texture can boil in i2v |

**Adopt H1-A.** Reason: the horizontal slit carries the concept ("one line") and leaves the cleanest field for the type. H1-B rejected as less distinctive and busier at the floor.
